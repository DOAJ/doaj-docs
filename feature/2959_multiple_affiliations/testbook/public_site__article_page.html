1. Test Article Landing Page Metadata and Affiliations Display

Test context
  • role: anonymous
ID Action Expected Result  
BV.1.1 Ensure there is a publicly available article in DOAJ with multiple authors, where at least one author has multiple affiliations and an ORCID iD.    
BV.1.2 Navigate to the article landing page at /article/ or search for the article on /search/articles and click on its title    
BV.1.2.1   The article title is displayed prominently at the top
BV.1.2.2   The journal title is displayed and links to the journal Table of Contents page
BV.1.2.3   The list of authors is displayed
BV.1.2.4   Publication date, volume, issue, and page numbers are displayed if provided
BV.1.2.5   DOI and Full-Text URL links are displayed and active
BV.1.2.6   Abstract and Keywords are displayed if provided
BV.1.3 Click on the "Affiliations" button/accordion under the authors list    
BV.1.3.1   The affiliations section expands
BV.1.3.2   Each author is listed with their respective affiliations
BV.1.3.3   Authors with multiple affiliations display each affiliation on a separate line
BV.1.3.4   Authors with ORCID iDs display a clickable ORCID icon/link to their ORCID profile
BV.1.4 Click on the "Affiliations" button/accordion again    
BV.1.4.1   The affiliations section collapses

3. Test Admin Edit Article Button on Article Landing Page

Test context
  • role: Admin
ID Action Expected Result  
BV.3.1 Log in with an admin account    
BV.3.2 Navigate to an article landing page at /article/    
BV.3.2.1   An "Edit this article" button is displayed near the top of the page
BV.3.3 Click the "Edit this article" button    
BV.3.3.1   The Admin Article Metadata form for this article opens in a new tab with the article's data pre-filled
BV.3.4 Log out of the admin account and view the same article landing page as an anonymous user or publisher    
BV.3.4.1   The "Edit this article" button is not visible
