1. Test Publisher Comments on New Applications and Update Requests

Setup:

Navigate to /testdrive/publisher_comment

At the top of the Testdrive page, the test title is shown followed by its ID, e.g. 'TestName (id: 234)'.

Usernames and record titles follow the `name__id` format. When the test says to log in as User__<id>, use the test ID, e.g. User__123.

During the test, you may see multiple records or users with the same name. Always select the one that matches your test ID.

All data in the Testdrive dashboard includes a copy button. You can use it to quickly copy the correct value and use it in the relevant place in the test.

Open a new session in a different browser or a private (incognito) window. Do not close the Testdrive page.
